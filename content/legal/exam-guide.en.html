What the state language test is

The test shows how well you know Latvian. There are six levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2. Many customer-facing jobs require at least level B1. [CHECK VIAA: which jobs and which regulation sets the required level]

The test is organised by the State Education Development Agency (VIAA). [CHECK VIAA: confirm the organiser is VIAA]

Structure of the B1 test

PartDuration
Listening40 min
Reading40 min
Writing40 min
Speaking10–15 min

Important: to pass, you need at least 60% of the maximum points in each part. If one part is below 60%, you do not pass, even if the other parts are excellent.

How to register

  1. Choose a test centre and date on the VIAA website. [CHECK VIAA: link to the schedule]
  2. Register [online / in person]. [CHECK VIAA: how to register and which documents are needed]
  3. Pay the fee (see below) and keep the payment confirmation.

Fee

  • The test fee is €52. [CHECK VIAA]
  • It must be paid at least 7 working days before the test. [CHECK VIAA]
  • Ukrainian civilians are exempt from the fee. [CHECK VIAA: conditions and documents needed]
  • If you do not pass, you pay again to retake. [CHECK VIAA: whether all parts must be retaken]

On the day

  • Bring an identity document. [CHECK VIAA: which documents are accepted]
  • Arrive on time. [CHECK VIAA: how early]
  • In the writing part, write clearly and stick to the word count.

Results

Results are available by the 7th working day after the test. [CHECK VIAA: where to see them and how the certificate is issued]

How to prepare

  • Find your weakest skill — that is what most often decides the result.
  • Practise all four parts, not only the ones you are good at.
  • In writing, always answer every point of the task and stick to the word count.
  • In speaking, use full sentences and give examples.
